# Illustrative Business Case & Strategy: ConsultingCraft AI

> All market, conversion, pricing, and cost figures below are assumptions for a
> student product exercise. They are not validated market research or forecasts.

## 1. Executive Summary
ConsultingCraft AI targets a highly motivated, high-willingness-to-pay segment of job seekers: those aiming for MBB (McKinsey, BCG, Bain) and elite Tech PM roles. By automating the most tedious and rule-bound aspects of resume writing, it provides immediate, tangible value.

## 2. Illustrative Market Sizing (TAM, SAM, SOM)
*   **Total Addressable Market (TAM):** ~3 million students and young professionals globally applying for competitive corporate roles annually.
*   **Serviceable Available Market (SAM):** ~500,000 candidates specifically targeting tier-1 management consulting, investment banking, and tech product management.
*   **Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM):** Capturing 2% of the SAM (10,000 users) in Year 1.

## 3. Competitive Analysis
*   **General Resume Builders (Zety, Canva, Novoresume):** 
    *   *Weakness:* Focus on aesthetic design (colors, two columns) rather than the strict, black-and-white, single-column, high-density format required by consulting.
*   **AI Resume Tailors (Teal, Rezi, Wonsulting):**
    *   *Weakness:* They optimize for ATS keywords but lack the "spatial awareness" to ensure perfect 1-line bullet points or strict MECE bucketing. They often result in text that wraps awkwardly.
*   **ConsultingCraft AI (Our Advantage):**
    *   *Moat:* The "Constraint Optimizer" agent that guarantees physical layout perfection (no trailing white space, exact 1-page fit) combined with elite-tier RAC rewriting.

## 4. Monetization Strategy (Business Model)
*   **Freemium Model:** 
    *   *Free Tier:* Basic RAC rewriting, standard margins, watermark on export.
    *   *Premium ($19.99/month or $49/lifetime):* Advanced spatial constraint optimizer, custom margins, multi-modal JD ingestion, PDF/Docx export without watermark.
*   **B2B / University Partnerships:** Selling site licenses to MBA career centers ($5k-$10k/year per school).

## 5. Illustrative Annual Operating Plan (AOP) - Year 1 Scenario
*   **Revenue:** 10,000 users * 15% conversion rate to Premium (1,500 users) * $49 LTV = **$73,500**
*   **Costs:** 
    *   LLM API Costs (OpenAI/Gemini): ~$0.10 per CV generation. Assuming 10 generations/user = $10,000.
    *   Hosting & Infrastructure (Vercel/Streamlit Cloud): $1,000.
*   **Net Profit:** ~$62,500 (85% gross margin).

## 6. Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy
To acquire our initial 10,000 users (SOM) with a low Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), we will execute a 3-pillar strategy:
*   **Pillar 1: B2B2C via Consulting Clubs:** Partner directly with the Presidents of Consulting Clubs at Top 25 MBA programs (e.g., Wharton, Booth, HBS). Offer their club members an exclusive 30-day premium trial to seed power users.
*   **Pillar 2: "Roast My Resume" Content Marketing:** Launch a TikTok/LinkedIn organic content series where we take a poorly formatted resume, run it through ConsultingCraft AI, and show the elite RAC-formatted output. The visual "before and after" is highly shareable.
*   **Pillar 3: Product-Led Growth (PLG):** The free tier embeds a subtle watermark ("Formatted perfectly by ConsultingCraft AI") at the bottom of the exported PDF. When users send these resumes to peers for review, it creates a viral loop.
